## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitor is a quiet household seeking a furnished, temporary home through the North Shuswap’s quieter fall, winter, and spring seasons, with a credible reason and timeline to leave at the end of the fixed term. Strong-fit situations include insurance displacement, a defined professional assignment, a local build or renovation, or a confirmed between-homes period.

## Product Purpose

Present Blake Point House as a real furnished quiet-season home—not a revolving vacation rental—and help a strong-fit visitor understand the property, the fall-through-spring living context, and the tenancy expectations before expressing interest.

Success means a qualified household completes a short expression-of-interest flow, downloads the detailed brochure, or contacts the owner directly by phone or text. The page should discourage party, event, recreation-group, and crew-accommodation use without screening people on protected personal characteristics.

## Positioning

A spacious furnished home in gated Blake Point offered as one continuous quiet-season tenancy, paired with a candid explanation of the house, the floor plan, the North Shuswap’s fall, winter, and spring character, and the practical realities of living there.

## Operating Context

Visitors will evaluate the home on desktop and mobile. The public page lives at `blakepoint.ca/rental/` on GitHub Pages. It should support a visual property tour, an MLS-sourced floor-plan explorer, an area map with nearby services and attractions, a downloadable brochure, a short pre-qualification/expression-of-interest form, and direct call/text contact.

## Product Principles

1. Let verified property evidence lead; do not fill asset gaps with claims.
2. Qualify for fit while preserving dignity and legal fairness.
3. Explain remote winter living honestly enough that unsuitable prospects self-select out.
4. Make exploration tactile and memorable without compromising speed, accessibility, or mobile use.
5. Keep owner privacy and unconfirmed community privileges protected.
6. Before publication, inspect original-resolution images for family names (including DREWLO), faces/minors, house numbers, access/security information, licence plates, personal photographs, mirrors/screens/reflections and documents; blur, crop or omit anything sensitive.
