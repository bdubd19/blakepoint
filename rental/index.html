CURRENT FURNISHING NOTES

How the rooms actually live.

The Matterport drawing remains unchanged as source evidence; these notes clarify the current furniture and use.
Floor 1 · basement

The shiplap room labeled Recreation Room has a pull-out sofa and TV and is used as a sleeping room. The bedroom beside the mechanical/electrical room has a bed. The rear room is shown as Bedroom on the Matterport drawing but has no closet; the purchase records treat it as non-conforming, and it is Brandon’s office with a desk and futon.

Floor 2 · main level

The primary—or master—bedroom is the home’s nicest bedroom. The main level also contains the kitchen, dining and living spaces, laundry, garage and deck.

Floor 3 · upstairs

Both upstairs bedrooms are furnished with double bunk beds. This level also includes the plan-labeled office, bathroom and balcony.

06

A GOOD RESIDENTIAL FIT

A home for living,
not an operating base.

What fits

  • One quiet residential tenancy of 90 days or longer
  • Ordinary remote work and day-to-day family life
  • Approved occupants, vehicles and pets confirmed in writing
  • Respect for strata parking, quiet hours and common property

What does not

  • Parties, events or group-recreation staging
  • Commercial or crew accommodation
  • Recreational-vehicle repair or trailering operations
  • Fuel storage or disruptive property uses

Before applying

  • Internet is included; verify performance for job-specific needs
  • Hydro and propane are extra
  • Dock, slip and amenity access are not promised unless written into the tenancy
  • Final terms use the BC RTB-1 plus a reviewed addendum
