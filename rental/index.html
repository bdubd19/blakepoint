07

A GOOD RESIDENTIAL FIT

A home for living,
not an operating base.

What fits

  • One quiet residential tenancy of 90 days or longer
  • Ordinary remote work and day-to-day family life
  • Approved occupants, vehicles and pets confirmed in writing
  • Respect for strata parking, quiet hours and common property

What does not

  • Parties, events or group-recreation staging
  • Commercial or crew accommodation
  • Recreational-vehicle repair or trailering operations
  • Fuel storage or disruptive property uses

Before applying

  • Internet is included; verify performance for job-specific needs
  • Hydro and propane are extra
  • Dock, slip and amenity access are not promised unless written into the tenancy
  • Final terms use the BC RTB-1 plus a reviewed addendum
